STEP 10 – THREE-SYSTEM OFDM BER COMPARISON

Objective:
To compare the BER performance of three OFDM receiver
configurations under a multipath channel.

System 1:
Multipath channel without equalization.

System 2:
Multipath channel with Zero-Forcing equalization using the
known channel frequency response.

System 3:
Multipath channel with pilot-based channel estimation followed
by Zero-Forcing equalization.

SNR Range:
0 to 20 dB

Comparison:

System 1 demonstrates the effect of multipath distortion without
channel compensation.

System 2 provides an ideal reference because the receiver uses
the known channel response.

System 3 estimates the channel using a transmitted pilot and
therefore represents a more practical channel-estimation approach.

Observation:
BER generally decreases as SNR increases for all three systems.

The pilot-based system can have higher BER than the known-channel
system because the estimated channel contains noise and estimation
error.

Conclusion:
The comparison demonstrates the importance of channel estimation
and equalization in OFDM communication systems.
