STEP 4 - OFDM RECEIVER

Objective:
To recover the transmitted QPSK data from the OFDM signal.

Receiver Process:
1. The received serial OFDM signal is divided into individual OFDM symbols.
2. The cyclic prefix is removed.
3. FFT converts the time-domain OFDM signal back to the frequency domain.
4. The recovered QPSK symbols are extracted.
5. QPSK demodulation converts the symbols back into binary data.
6. The recovered bits are compared with the transmitted bits.
7. BER is calculated.

Channel:
An ideal channel was used in Step 4 without noise or multipath effects.

Result:
The transmitted data was successfully recovered at the receiver.
The BER was approximately zero under the ideal channel condition.
The received QPSK constellation was also successfully obtained.
