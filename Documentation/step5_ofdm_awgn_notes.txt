STEP 5 - OFDM BER PERFORMANCE OVER AWGN CHANNEL

Objective:
To evaluate the BER performance of the complete OFDM system
under different AWGN channel conditions.

Process:
1. Random binary data is generated.
2. The data is QPSK modulated.
3. QPSK symbols are arranged into OFDM symbols.
4. IFFT converts the symbols into the time domain.
5. A cyclic prefix is added.
6. AWGN noise is introduced at different SNR levels.
7. The cyclic prefix is removed at the receiver.
8. FFT converts the received signal back to the frequency domain.
9. QPSK demodulation recovers the transmitted bits.
10. BER is calculated for each SNR value.

SNR Range:
0 dB to 20 dB with a 2 dB step.

Result:
The BER decreases as the SNR increases.
This demonstrates the effect of AWGN noise on the
performance of the OFDM communication system.
