STEP 7 – CHANNEL ESTIMATION AND EQUALIZATION

Project:
5G OFDM Modem

Objective:
To reduce the effect of multipath channel distortion using
channel frequency response and Zero-Forcing equalization.

Parameters:
FFT Size = 64
Cyclic Prefix = 16
SNR Range = 0 to 20 dB
Channel Delays = [0 2 4]
Channel Gains = [1 0.5 0.25]
Modulation = QPSK

Process:
1. Generate random binary data.
2. Perform QPSK modulation.
3. Arrange QPSK symbols into OFDM symbols.
4. Perform IFFT.
5. Add cyclic prefix.
6. Transmit the signal through a multipath channel.
7. Add AWGN noise.
8. Remove the cyclic prefix at the receiver.
9. Perform FFT.
10. Calculate the channel frequency response.
11. Apply Zero-Forcing equalization.
12. Perform QPSK demodulation.
13. Calculate BER for different SNR values.

Outputs:
1. OFDM BER with Channel Estimation and Equalization.
2. Channel Frequency Response.

Important Note:
This step uses the known channel impulse response to calculate
the channel frequency response. Therefore, this is a known-channel
or perfect-channel-response equalization demonstration, rather
than a true pilot-based channel estimation system.
