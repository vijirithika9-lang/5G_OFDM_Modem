STEP 8 – BER COMPARISON BEFORE AND AFTER EQUALIZATION

Objective:
To compare the BER performance of the OFDM system before and
after Zero-Forcing (ZF) equalization.

Step 6:
OFDM system with multipath channel and AWGN without equalization.

Step 7:
OFDM system with multipath channel, AWGN and ZF equalization
using the known channel frequency response.

SNR Range:
0 to 20 dB

Observation:
The BER decreases as SNR increases.

The system with ZF equalization provides significantly lower BER
than the system without equalization for the simulated multipath
channel.

Example:
At 14 dB SNR:
Without equalization BER = 0.016485
With ZF equalization BER = 0.000450

At 20 dB SNR:
Without equalization BER = 0.000900
With ZF equalization BER = 0.000000

Conclusion:
Channel equalization effectively compensates for the frequency
selective distortion introduced by the multipath channel.

Important Note:
Step 7 uses the known channel response. It is not yet a true
pilot-based channel estimation system.
