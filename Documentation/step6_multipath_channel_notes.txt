STEP 6 - OFDM OVER MULTIPATH FADING CHANNEL

Objective:
To evaluate the performance of the OFDM communication system
when the transmitted signal experiences multipath propagation.

Channel Configuration:
Path delays: 0, 2 and 4 samples
Path gains: 1.00, 0.50 and 0.25

Process:
1. QPSK symbols are generated from random binary data.
2. The QPSK symbols are converted into OFDM symbols using IFFT.
3. A cyclic prefix is added.
4. The transmitted signal is passed through a multipath channel.
5. AWGN noise is added at different SNR levels.
6. The cyclic prefix is removed at the receiver.
7. FFT is applied to recover the frequency-domain symbols.
8. QPSK demodulation is performed.
9. BER is calculated for different SNR values.

Result:
The OFDM system was successfully tested under a multipath
channel with AWGN noise. The channel impulse response and
BER performance were obtained.
